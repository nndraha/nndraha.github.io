# Dr. Nodali Ndraha
*Principal Investigator*

<b>Research focus</b>: Applying molecular diagnostics and synthetic biology, including CRISPR-Cas12a technologies, to improve pathogen detection in Indonesian food matrices. Alongside my active laboratory research and drafting a book on foodborne bacterial pathogens in Indonesia, I serve as the Editor-in-Chief for *Trophos Science of Food*, published by NUSAXIS PUSTAKA MANDIRI LLC., where I manage the editorial process and develop new publishing initiatives.
