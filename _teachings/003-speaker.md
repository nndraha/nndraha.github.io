## Overview
As part of my commitment to advancing food safety and public health, I am available to deliver keynote speeches, guest lectures, and specialized presentations or workshops. I welcome invitations from public institutions, private companies, academic organizations, and industry groups seeking expert insights.

I am available to speak on a variety of topics, specifically focusing on management, research, and innovation in microbial food safety. This includes discussions on quantitative microbial risk assessment (QMRA), predictive microbiology, pathogen detection technologies, and strategic approaches to mitigating foodborne risks in both industrial and regulatory contexts.
