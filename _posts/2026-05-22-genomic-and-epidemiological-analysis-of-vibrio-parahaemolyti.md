### The Quick Summary

A tiny germ called *Vibrio parahaemolyticus* often makes people sick from eating seafood. A new type of this germ, called O10:K4, has quickly become a big problem in China since around 2020. Scientists are studying these germs from sick people to understand how they spread and cause illness.

---

### Original Abstract

> Vibrio parahaemolyticus is a leading cause of seafood-associated bacterial gastroenteritis, which poses increasing public health challenges worldwide. Since its emergence in approximately 2020, VP O10:K4 has rapidly become the dominant cause of gastroenteritis in China.
