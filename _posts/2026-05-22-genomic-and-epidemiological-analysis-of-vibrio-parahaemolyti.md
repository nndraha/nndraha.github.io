### The Quick Summary

A tiny germ called *Vibrio* can make people really sick, especially if they eat seafood. A new type of this germ, named O10:K4, started making many people in China sick around 2020. Scientists are now studying this new germ's DNA to understand why it's spreading so fast and how to stop it.

---

### Original Abstract

> Vibrio parahaemolyticus is a leading cause of seafood-associated bacterial gastroenteritis, which poses increasing public health challenges worldwide. Since its emergence in approximately 2020, VP O10:K4 has rapidly become the dominant cause of gastroenteritis in China.
