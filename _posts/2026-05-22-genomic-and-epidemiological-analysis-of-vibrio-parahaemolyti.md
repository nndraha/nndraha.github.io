### The Quick Summary

Sometimes, tiny germs in seafood can make people really sick to their stomachs. Recently, a new, tougher kind of this germ has popped up and is making lots more people sick from seafood, especially in places like China. Scientists are studying this germ closely to figure out how to stop it and keep all our delicious seafood safe to eat!

### Original Abstract

> Vibrio parahaemolyticus is a leading cause of seafood-associated bacterial gastroenteritis, which poses increasing public health challenges worldwide. Since its emergence in approximately 2020, VP O10:K4 has rapidly become the dominant cause of gastroenteritis in China.
