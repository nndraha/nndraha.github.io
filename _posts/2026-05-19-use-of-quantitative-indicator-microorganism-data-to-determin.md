### The Quick Summary

A germ called Salmonella can make people sick, especially from turkey. Factories check turkeys for this germ at certain steps to keep our food safe. This study wants to know if those early checks really help predict if the ground turkey we eat has the germ.

---

### Original Abstract

In the United States, Salmonella oversight in poultry relies on USDA-FSIS verification testing, which requires sampling 1 out of every 3,000 birds at re-hang and post-chill to assess process control.
