### The Quick Summary

*Salmonella enterica* is a germ often found in turkey that can make people very sick. To stop this, people check turkeys at different steps, like when birds are first hung up or after they've been chilled. This study wants to find out if these early checks are a good way to tell if the final ground turkey will have *Salmonella enterica* and be unsafe.
