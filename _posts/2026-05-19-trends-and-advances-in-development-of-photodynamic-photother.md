### The Quick Summary

Imagine if your food stayed fresh for much longer! Scientists are making super smart food wrappers that use special light tricks to keep food safe. This helps stop tiny germs from spoiling our food, so we waste less and always have yummy, healthy meals!

### Original Abstract

> Food packaging has evolved as advanced technologies to reduce food wastage by actively enhancing safety, quality, and sustainability. This review focuses on the mechanisms, materials chemistry, and application performance of photodynamic, photothermal, and intelligent food packaging systems that exploit light-induced chemical and thermal processes for microbial control and freshness monitoring.
