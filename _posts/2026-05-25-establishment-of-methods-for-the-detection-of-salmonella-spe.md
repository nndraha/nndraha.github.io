### The Quick Summary

This study made new, accurate tests to find tiny germs called *Salmonella*. They used special methods called PCR and qPCR to look for the germs. These tests help us know if animals or people have *Salmonella* so we can keep everyone safe and healthy.

### Practical Implications

This study provides food scientists with a highly accurate and reliable method to detect *Salmonella* bacteria. This improved detection capability is crucial for ensuring food safety, preventing contamination in the supply chain, and reducing the risk of foodborne illnesses.

### Potential Use in Indonesia

These findings are highly relevant for Indonesia, where *Salmonella* contamination can be a significant concern, especially in traditional markets and street food vendors due to tropical climates and varying hygiene standards. Implementing such a specific and sensitive detection method can enhance food safety monitoring in local supply chains, from farm to fork. This would help prevent outbreaks of foodborne illnesses and protect public health across the archipelago.
